# Bailamos friendship pricing and working agreement

Draft for discussion — September 2026  
All amounts are net and exclude VAT where applicable. This is a commercial working draft, not legal or tax advice.

## Purpose

This agreement gives the restaurant a reduced start-up price while it is new, but clearly defines the developer’s responsibilities, working hours and future pricing. Friendship does not mean unlimited or permanently unpaid work.

## 1. Normal value and friendship discount

- Normal estimated value of the current production-launch package: **€9,500**
- Friendship/start-up price: **€4,000**
- The discount should appear clearly on the proposal and invoice so the client understands the real value.

Suggested payment schedule:

- €1,000 when production preparation begins
- €1,000 when the website launches
- €1,000 one month after launch
- €1,000 three months after launch

## 2. What the €4,000 launch package includes

- Final production deployment of the existing website and administration system
- Replacement of demonstration restaurant details with client-supplied information
- Final menu, event, social and media configuration using client-supplied content
- Production database migrations
- Domain and hosting configuration
- Reservation confirmation and cancellation email setup
- One complete production booking test
- Reasonable mobile, desktop, accessibility and performance verification
- Correction of defects in the agreed launch scope for 30 days after launch
- Two structured client review rounds

It does not include photography, ongoing social posting, advertising management, unlimited content changes, new features, legal advice or third-party subscription charges.

## 3. Start-up support period

For the restaurant’s first six months after launch:

### Essential maintenance — €400/month

Includes up to **5 hours per month**:

- Website and reservation-system health checks
- Backup and security review
- Small menu, image, event and opening-hours updates
- Basic email-delivery monitoring
- Minor corrections and owner support

Unused hours expire at the end of each month. Work beyond five hours requires written approval and is billed at the discounted rates below.

## 4. Discounted hourly rates during the start-up period

| Work type | Friend/start-up rate |
| --- | ---: |
| General website maintenance and content updates | €50/hour |
| Menu, events, gallery and social-link updates | €50/hour |
| SEO/GEO review and optimization | €55/hour |
| Email campaign setup and testing | €55/hour |
| AI-assisted copy, captions and content preparation | €55/hour |
| Image selection, resizing, cropping and web optimization | €55/hour |
| AI image generation or complex image editing | €60/hour plus API cost |
| Video preparation, captions and web/social optimization | €60/hour |
| New website features, integrations or database work | €65/hour |
| Urgent work outside agreed hours | €80/hour |

Minimum billable unit: 30 minutes. Provide a short monthly time report showing the date, request, time and result.

## 5. External costs

The restaurant pays external services directly whenever possible:

- Domain and hosting
- Email provider
- OpenAI or other AI API usage
- Social scheduling tools
- Advertising spend
- Stock media or music licences
- Professional photography or video production

Do not finance advertising from the developer’s personal account. Agree a monthly cap before any paid campaign begins.

Suggested initial external budget:

| Item | Monthly limit |
| --- | ---: |
| Hosting, email and AI | €50 |
| Test advertising | €150–€300 |
| Optional paid media/tools | Only with approval |

## 6. Approval and responsibility

The restaurant owner must approve:

- Prices, allergens and menu facts
- Event dates, conditions and capacity
- Promotional offers and advertising budget
- Email audience and campaign content
- Social posts
- AI-generated or AI-edited media
- Legal, privacy and imprint text

AI may prepare drafts but cannot publish automatically. The developer is responsible for implementing the approved technical workflow, not for guaranteeing rankings, followers, bookings or revenue.

## 7. Stable-income review

Pricing is reviewed after **six months**, or earlier when the restaurant has achieved stable operating income for **three consecutive months**.

“Stable income” must be agreed using a measurable indicator chosen by the owner—for example positive operating cash flow or a monthly revenue target. The developer should not request access to unnecessary financial data; written confirmation from the owner is sufficient unless both parties agree otherwise.

After the review, choose one normal commercial arrangement:

### Option A — hourly support

- Standard website/content work: **€85/hour**
- SEO, email, AI and media work: **€95/hour**
- New features and integrations: **€105/hour**
- Urgent/out-of-hours work: **€130/hour**
- No monthly minimum; work is scheduled subject to availability

### Option B — Growth Operations retainer

- **€1,200/month**
- Up to 10 hours of planned work
- Technical maintenance
- One coordinated campaign or event update
- SEO/GEO and analytics review
- Email preparation and delivery check
- Additional work: €105/hour

### Option C — Managed Digital Partner

- **€2,250/month**
- Up to 20 hours of planned work
- Campaign calendar
- Website, email and social coordination
- AI/content review
- Monthly performance report
- Priority support during agreed business hours
- Additional work: €115/hour

## 8. How work is requested

Every request should be sent in writing and include:

- What must change
- Required content/files
- Desired deadline
- Where it should be published
- Who approves it

The developer replies with one of:

- Included in the monthly allowance
- Estimated number of hours and cost
- Separate fixed-price proposal
- Cannot begin because required content or approval is missing

No substantial work begins without written approval of the estimate.

## 9. Boundaries

- Support hours and response times must be written down.
- Maintenance does not mean permanent 24/7 availability.
- New functionality is not a defect fix.
- Third-party platform changes are separately estimated.
- Client delays move the delivery date.
- The client confirms rights to supplied images, videos, logos and music.
- Either party may end the monthly arrangement with 30 days’ written notice.
- On termination, unpaid invoices remain due and client-owned data is handed over in an agreed format.

## 10. Simple wording for the conversation

> The system’s normal launch value is approximately €9,500. Because you are my close friend and the restaurant is new, I am offering a €4,000 start-up price with flexible milestone payments. After launch, €400 per month includes up to five hours of maintenance and small updates. Extra work is estimated and approved before I begin. After six months, or after three stable-income months, we review and move to normal commercial rates. Hosting, advertising and other platforms are paid directly by the restaurant.

## 11. Sign-off checklist

Before signing, both parties should fill in:

- Legal name and address of each party
- Project start and target launch date
- Exact deliverables
- Payment dates
- VAT treatment
- Support hours and communication channel
- Definition of urgent work
- Stable-income review date
- Client approval contact
- Data-processing and privacy responsibilities
- Ownership/licensing of source code, content and accounts
- Termination and handover process

Have a qualified German legal/tax professional review the final contract and invoices before use.
