# Bailamos Digital Growth Platform

## Client education, roadmap and commercial proposal

Prepared: September 2026  
Pricing: net, excluding VAT and third-party charges

## 1. Executive summary

Bailamos is not receiving only a restaurant website. The proposed product is a connected digital operating and marketing platform:

- A customer website that explains the restaurant and converts visits into reservations
- A live menu, events, gallery and video experience
- A reservation system with availability, table selection, confirmation and cancellation
- An owner dashboard for bookings, tables, opening hours, menu and content
- SEO for Google and local discovery
- GEO for accurate discovery by AI assistants
- Permission-based email marketing
- Social campaign preparation for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok
- A future Content Agent that turns approved restaurant media and notes into owner-reviewed drafts
- Measurement showing which activity creates reservations instead of only likes

The commercial goal is to create more qualified reservations, reduce manual administration, promote events consistently and build a customer audience the restaurant controls.

## 2. What SEO, GEO and digital marketing provide

### SEO

Search Engine Optimization helps Google understand the restaurant, menu, location, opening hours and events. It improves the opportunity to appear for relevant local searches and ensures search previews are accurate. Rankings are influenced by competition, reviews, reputation and external signals, so no ethical supplier can guarantee a particular ranking.

### GEO

Generative Engine Optimization makes the same verified information clear to AI assistants. It includes structured restaurant, menu and event data; consistent business facts; useful descriptions; and machine-readable sources. GEO improves clarity and eligibility for AI answers but cannot guarantee that a third-party AI system will mention Bailamos.

### Digital marketing

Digital marketing turns current restaurant activity into coordinated website, social and email campaigns. Its value is consistency, faster production, reuse of approved content and measurable reservation outcomes.

## 4. Event campaign process

### Step 1 — event brief

The restaurant supplies verified facts:

- Name, date and time
- Price or minimum spend, when applicable
- Capacity and booking deadline
- Menu, performer or activity details
- Cancellation terms
- Approved photographs and video

### Step 2 — campaign preparation

The system or Content Agent prepares drafts for:

- Website event page
- Homepage announcement
- SEO title and description
- Structured Event data for search and AI services
- Instagram and Facebook captions
- TikTok caption and short-video concept
- Newsletter subject, preview text and body
- Image alt text

### Step 3 — owner approval

The owner reviews every factual claim and chooses which channels to use. AI does not publish, change prices or invent availability.

### Step 4 — recommended schedule

- 3–4 weeks before: initial announcement
- 2 weeks before: details and first newsletter
- 7 days before: social reminder
- 2–3 days before: remaining availability, only when factually true
- Event day: story reminder
- Following day: approved photographs, thank-you and next-event invitation

### Step 5 — measurement

Report website visits, event-page visits, reservation starts, completed reservations, email clicks, social referral traffic and cancellations.

## 5. Future Content Agent

### Owner workflow

1. Upload an approved folder to a private Admin Inbox.
2. Scan files for type, size, safety and duplicates.
3. Analyze images, selected video frames, transcripts, filenames and notes.
4. Classify content as menu, event, gallery, social or unrelated.
5. Prepare captions, descriptions, alt text, SEO and suggested placements.
6. Display all suggestions in a review queue.
7. Allow approval by field or by item.
8. Publish only approved changes.
9. Record who published each change and provide rollback.

The hosted website cannot access arbitrary folders on the owner’s computer. Folder intake must use an Admin upload or a separately installed utility limited to one owner-approved directory.

### Media capabilities

- Resolution, blur, exposure and crop assessment
- Website, portrait and story crop suggestions
- Web-optimized copies while retaining originals
- Image alt text
- Video frame selection and speech transcription
- Caption and short-form edit suggestions
- Quality warnings before publication

Generative edits to food photography must be separately approved and clearly identified to avoid misrepresenting real dishes.

### Recommended proposal to this client

For a small independent restaurant, offer the project in stages rather than quoting the maximum scope immediately:

- **Production launch package:** €9,500 net
- **Growth package: SEO/GEO, analytics and email:** additional €7,500 net
- **Content Agent package:** additional €9,500 net
- **Advanced social/media integrations:** quoted after platform-account review, normally €6,000–€10,000 net

Recommended full planned value: approximately **€32,500–€36,500 net**, paid by milestones.

This recommendation aligns with a specialist blended rate of roughly €95–€115 per hour. Current published German benchmarks put web-development freelance work around €91/hour and the wider 2026 freelance market average around €103/hour. Fixed pricing must also cover project management, testing, revisions, risk and warranty—not only typing time.

### Payment schedule

- 40% at contract signature
- 30% when the production-ready feature set is demonstrated
- 20% after client content and acceptance testing
- 10% at production launch

Each future phase should have its own signed statement of work. Additional requests outside the agreed scope are billed at **€105/hour net**.

## 11. Client responsibilities

The client must provide and approve:

- Menu names, prices, ingredients and allergens
- Event facts, capacity, terms and availability
- Rights to every supplied photograph, video, logo and music track
- Social-platform accounts and required business verification
- Sending domain and lawful email subscriber consent
- Imprint, privacy and legal text reviewed by a qualified professional
- Timely approvals and one responsible decision maker
- Direct payment method for third-party accounts

Delays in content, account approval or client feedback change the delivery schedule.

## 12. Supplier responsibilities and limits

The supplier is responsible for implementing and operating the agreed technical system with professional care. The contract should explicitly exclude:

- Guaranteed Google ranking or AI recommendation
- Guaranteed booking or revenue level
- Social-platform approval or uninterrupted external APIs
- Legal advice or legal approval of privacy/marketing practices
- Client-supplied copyright or factual errors
- Incidents caused by credentials or accounts controlled by the client
- Work outside contracted support hours
- Unlimited revisions or unlimited content production

Include a 30-day defect warranty for the agreed launch scope. New features, content changes and third-party changes are maintenance or change requests.

## 13. Reporting and business benefit

The monthly report should focus on business outcomes:

- Completed reservations
- Reservation conversion rate
- Event reservations
- Cancellation and no-show rates
- Menu and event engagement
- Calls and map clicks
- Newsletter growth, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes
- Social referrals that lead to reservations
- Returning customer indicators where lawful

Do not promise revenue without the restaurant’s real numbers. Use this return formula:

```text
Monthly value created
= additional completed covers × average contribution per cover
− campaign and platform costs
```

For example, the client supplies its own average contribution per cover; the dashboard then shows how many additional completed covers are required to cover the monthly retainer.
